# Services

## Headless services 
- when you want to communicate with one specific pod 
- headless services is used for stateful app, sateful app pod are not similear.

```yaml
apiVersion: v1
Kind: services 
metadata: 
    name: mongodb-service-headless
spec:
    type: NodePort
    clusterIP: None 
    selecter: 
        app: mongodb
    ports: 
        - protocol: TCP
        port: 27017
        targetport: 27017

```
